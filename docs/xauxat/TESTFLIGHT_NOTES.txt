This build brings together the reviewed messaging, voice, photo and privacy updates.

What to test:
- Exchange text, photos, files and voice messages in both directions between two devices on Wi-Fi and mobile data. Check arrival time, attachment playback readiness, duplicate items and send status after a connection interruption.
- Open a chat with several pending attachments and confirm automatic receiving continues through the whole batch.
- Record, pause, resume, preview and send voice notes. Try Voice Mask with Rain or Outside, then turn Voice Mask off and confirm the saved background sound is not applied.
- Import stickers using Photos, switch between Stickers and Soundboard, and check search, favorites and recent items.
- Decline an incoming call and confirm the caller stops ringing. Check call cleanup after a failed connection.
- Background and reopen the app with authentication enabled. Confirm immediate locking, correct profile selection and recovery of the same conversation session.
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, briefly enable airplane mode, then restore connectivity. Verify incoming and outgoing delivery recover without force-closing the app.
- If startup or delivery stalls, use Private connection > Copy connection diagnostics and include the time, network type and affected media in the report.

Known limitation: when redundant relay queues are enabled, a full relay can make a contact appear inactive even when another relay is delivering receipts. Please report this status along with the diagnostics.

Physical iPhone acceptance is still needed for delivery latency, real audio playback and call/notification behavior. Automated relay tests are not a latency guarantee.

Build 359 improves incoming-message recovery after opening or unlocking XauXat. The app now checks receive subscriptions after every global reconnection and continues recovery for failed relays without needing an outgoing message. It retains the Tor, call-audio, Vault, Stealth and messaging updates from build 358.

What to test on two iPhones:
- Leave the receiving phone in the background, send several messages from the other phone, then open and unlock XauXat. Wait for the private connection to recover without sending anything from the receiving phone. Check that the queued messages arrive.
- Repeat after locking the phone, after a longer background interval, and with app authentication enabled. Confirm the correct profile and existing conversation reopen.
- Repeat in both directions on Wi-Fi and mobile data, including a network change or a brief airplane-mode interruption. Check incoming and outgoing messages, attachments, duplicates and delivery status.
- Check notifications while XauXat is in the background and closed. Note whether notification delivery and message arrival after opening differ.
- Place and answer audio calls in both directions, including with Stealth voice effects, and check audio after the call connects.
- If reception stalls, copy Private connection > Copy connection diagnostics before sending from the affected phone. Record the build, time and network type, then compare with diagnostics after recovery.

Automated checks reproduce and cover the incoming-only recovery defect. Device-specific delivery latency and background Apple push notifications still require physical iPhone acceptance.

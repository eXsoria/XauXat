This build focuses on private-connection startup and recovery on real iPhones.

What to test:
- Open XauXat repeatedly on Wi-Fi and on mobile data. Confirm the existing profile and chats open after Tor becomes ready.
- Send and receive text, photos and voice messages in both directions between two devices.
- Briefly tap the microphone and confirm no voice draft remains. Interrupt a short hold before recording starts and confirm it leaves no draft. Hold until recording begins, release to preview, then resume and send; audio should only be sent when you press Send.
- Stay on the chat list with no conversation selected and no outgoing messages. Confirm incoming delivery continues after a temporary connection failure.
- Leave the app, lock the phone, then return without force-closing XauXat. Confirm the same session resumes and both sending and receiving recover.
- Switch Wi-Fi/mobile data and briefly enable airplane mode, then restore connectivity. Check recovery while the app is open and after returning to it.
- Repeat with app authentication and protected profiles. After backgrounding, enter the correct PIN for the same profile and confirm both receiving and sending resume. Recovery should respect the selected profile and unlock state.
- If opening stalls, try Refresh Tor connection and record its exact error. If Settings is accessible, use Private connection > Copy connection diagnostics before force-closing the app; the session journal resets when the process exits.

Automated validation covers startup retries with the existing database, foreground/session recovery, native subscription events without an open conversation, network/profile/store/authentication gates, and real Tor control-socket behavior. Physical iPhone acceptance is still required to confirm the reported intermittent incident.

Support privacy policy

    
This policy covers requests deliberately submitted to XauXat support, separate from your encrypted conversations. Contact: {{CONTACT}}.

    
We receive the request type, chosen reason, description you write, submission time and a random request reference. Contact names and selected message text are optional and are sent only when you explicitly include them. Files, photographs, full histories, profile identifiers, cryptographic keys and APNs tokens are not automatically attached.

    
Support requests are readable by the authorised operator to investigate problems, assess reports and reply. A report is an allegation, not independently verified evidence. We do not automatically ban people or identify private participants from their display names.

    
The app sends through its managed Tor connection on supported iOS versions. Opening this website in a browser uses that browser's network configuration. HTTPS protects transport. We do not set tracking cookies or use analytics. Connections are processed transiently for TLS and abuse limits; request access logs are disabled.

    
New-request email alerts contain only a random reference, not report content. Request data and replies expire {{DAYS}} days after the latest activity. Operators must also apply this retention to backups. To request earlier deletion, contact the address above and provide the reference and private receipt through an agreed secure channel.

    
Keep your private receipt: the service has no account-based recovery. Never send it to the contact you are reporting. You may also use the official contact address if the service is unavailable; your email provider will then process the message under its own policy.

    
Policy version: 2026-10-15. This is not the general XauXat app privacy policy.

  

  

    
Community rules and report handling

    
Do not use XauXat for harassment, threats, exploitation, illegal content, fraud or spam. Do not disclose someone else's private information without a lawful reason.

    
Report a contact or specific content from the app, or use this form. Blocking remains a separate immediate local action and does not require making a report. Include only the details needed for assessment.

    
An operator reviews incoming requests, prioritises safety concerns and replies through the private receipt. Actions may include guidance on local blocking, investigation of infrastructure we control, or appropriate escalation. End-to-end encryption and the lack of central user accounts limit central identification and removal; support cannot read unrelated conversations.

    
This service does not provide emergency response. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger.
